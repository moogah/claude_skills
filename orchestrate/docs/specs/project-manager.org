* Purpose

The PM reads where a change stands once a cycle and writes the PM digest: the cycle's counts, the
signals they fire, the questions they raise and a recommendation on goal drift, so that the user can
see whether the change is converging on its goals. Every number in the digest comes from the change's
record; the PM's own words only frame them. On a cascade it starts an Architect conformance run over
the cluster. It writes no code, no task file and no ask file, and decides nothing: when the PM runs
and what becomes of its digest are the integrate phase's, how a candidate question becomes an ask is
the =asks= capability's, and how the counts are computed is the =record= capability's.

* Requirements

** Requirement: Every number in the digest comes from the change's record
:PROPERTIES:
:GOAL:     none stated
:END:
The PM SHALL take every number its digest shows from the counts computed over the change's record for
the cycle, and SHALL write only the words around them: the framing of each signal, the candidate
questions, the goal-drift recommendation, the trends to watch and the meta-discoveries.

*** Scenario: Every number in the digest is one the record's counts give
- *WHEN* a PM digest is written
- *THEN* every number in its tables, its main-path readout and its asks table's columns other than the question is one the counts computed over the change's record give for the cycle
- *AND* the PM's own words are only the framing of each signal, the candidate questions, the goal-drift recommendation, the trends to watch and the meta-discoveries

*** Scenario: The computed counts outlast a failed prose pass
- *WHEN* the PM's prose pass fails
- *THEN* the counts and the signals computed for the cycle are still in the cycle's directory in the home

*** Scenario: A zero rejection rate beside inline fixes is not read as a clean batch
- *WHEN* the cycle's counts show no rejection and several inline fixes
- *THEN* the digest does not call the batch clean

** Requirement: The digest is one file per cycle, in a fixed form
:PROPERTIES:
:GOAL:     none stated
:END:
The PM SHALL write one digest per cycle, as a file in the cycle's directory, in a fixed form: a header
naming the change, the cycle, the time and the history window; the throughput, main-path and agents
readouts; a Signals section that is never empty, each line marked actionable, healthy or
informational; the asks for the user; the trends to watch; the meta-discoveries; and the goal-drift
check.

*** Scenario: The digest is one file in the cycle's directory
- *WHEN* the PM produces a cycle's digest
- *THEN* it is the file =pm-digest.md= in the cycle's directory in the home

*** Scenario: The PM hands back one line of its signals and its digest's path
- *WHEN* the PM's agent pass ends
- *THEN* it hands back one line of at most 200 characters, =pm <cycle-id>: <s> signals (<w> ⚠), <q> candidate questions[, conformance run <id>]; <path>=, the path of the digest relative to the home

*** Scenario: The digest opens with its change, cycle, time and history window
- *WHEN* a PM digest is written
- *THEN* its title names the change and the cycle
- *AND* it states when it was produced and the history window it reads

*** Scenario: The digest's sections come in the form's order
- *WHEN* a digest is written
- *THEN* its sections are, in order: Throughput, Critical path, Agents, Signals, Asks for the user, Trends to watch, Meta-discoveries and Goal-drift check

*** Scenario: The digest's Signals section is never empty
- *WHEN* a PM digest is written
- *THEN* it has a =Signals= section holding at least one signal line

*** Scenario: Each signal line is marked actionable, healthy or informational
- *WHEN* the digest shows a signal
- *THEN* its line is marked ⚠ when it is actionable, ✓ when the dimension is healthy, or ◦ when it is a trend to watch and not yet actionable

*** Scenario: A healthy tracked dimension is shown, not left out
- *WHEN* a dimension the digest tracks is healthy this cycle
- *THEN* the digest shows it with a ✓ line

*** Scenario: The digest carries trends to watch, meta-discoveries and a goal-drift check
- *WHEN* a PM digest is written
- *THEN* it has a section of trends to watch, a section of the cycle's meta-discoveries and a goal-drift check

** Requirement: The digest shows the cycle's throughput, its main path and its agents
:PROPERTIES:
:GOAL:     none stated
:END:
The digest SHALL show the throughput of this cycle and of each cycle in the history window, the state
of the change's main path, and each role's agents with their spend beside the quality of the work,
and SHALL read spend against quality in one line without proposing a change to any agent setting.

*** Scenario: The throughput table covers the history window
- *WHEN* a PM digest is written
- *THEN* it shows, for this cycle and each cycle of the history window, the tasks created, carried, completed, reviewed and rejected, the inline fixes, the tasks externalised, and the drainage

*** Scenario: The throughput table holds the form's counts only
- *WHEN* a PM digest's throughput table is written
- *THEN* its rows are those counts and the drainage, and no other
- *AND* the counts of tasks started, blocked, failed and done stay in the computed output

*** Scenario: The main-path readout counts active, blocked and complete tasks on the main path
- *WHEN* a PM digest is written
- *THEN* it shows the main path's active tasks against all active tasks
- *AND* the main path's blocked tasks, with what blocks them
- *AND* the main path's complete tasks against all its tasks, a total that counts the main path's task files no batch holds yet

*** Scenario: The digest shows spend by role beside quality by the Implementor's setting
- *WHEN* a PM digest is written
- *THEN* its agents section shows, for each role, its agents, the agent settings they ran at and their spend
- *AND* for each Implementor setting, its tasks, the rejections, the rework tasks and the defects found after done

*** Scenario: Spend is read against quality in one line
- *WHEN* a PM digest is written
- *THEN* its agents section has one agent-settings line reading the spend against the history beside the quality counts

*** Scenario: Spend that fell while quality fell is flagged
- *WHEN* a quality count rose for a role or setting whose spend fell
- *THEN* the agent-settings line is marked ⚠

*** Scenario: An agent that ran at another setting than it was spawned at is flagged
- *WHEN* the change's record shows an agent that ran at another agent setting than the one it was spawned at
- *THEN* the agent-settings line is marked ⚠ and names it

*** Scenario: The PM proposes no change to an agent setting
- *WHEN* a PM digest is written
- *THEN* it proposes no change to any agent setting

*** Scenario: The digest counts the whole-change review's findings
- *WHEN* the whole-change review ran in the cycle
- *THEN* the digest's counts of findings and corrections include that review's

** Requirement: Each signal fires on its threshold and names what it recommends
:PROPERTIES:
:GOAL:     none stated
:END:
The PM SHALL fire each signal when its condition holds against the project's threshold, or the
default where the project sets none, and SHALL name with it what the signal recommends; on a cascade
it SHALL start an Architect conformance run over the cluster instead of raising a question.

*** Scenario: A threshold the project does not set takes its default
- *WHEN* the project's configuration sets no threshold for a signal
- *THEN* the signal reads its default: a history window of 5 cycles; drainage below 1.0 for 3 consecutive cycles; more than 3 tasks discovered from one task; a task stale after 3 cycles; tasks awaiting review more than 1.5 times those being implemented; the cross-change backlog growing over 3 cycles

*** Scenario: Throughput inversion fires on falling drainage
- *WHEN* tasks completed over tasks created stays below the drainage threshold for the threshold's number of consecutive cycles, created counting no carried task and a cycle that created none left out of the series
- *THEN* the digest carries a ⚠ throughput signal

*** Scenario: A cascade fires when one task has too many tasks discovered from it
- *WHEN* more tasks than the cascade threshold were discovered from one task
- *THEN* the digest carries a ⚠ cascade signal naming that task and how many tasks were discovered from it

*** Scenario: A cascade starts an Architect conformance run, not a question
- *WHEN* the cascade signal fires
- *THEN* the PM spawns an Architect conformance run scoped to the cluster of the source task and the tasks discovered from it
- *AND* no candidate question is raised for the cascade

*** Scenario: The cascade run's brief names the cluster and its mission
- *WHEN* the PM spawns a conformance run for a cascade
- *THEN* its brief names the cluster, the source task, the tasks discovered from it, their discovered classes and the modules the diffs implicate
- *AND* asks which seam rows the cluster's tasks cite, whether the merged code matches them, which rows need amending and whether a seam is missing
- *AND* asks for every defect found as a finding with its kind and severity

*** Scenario: The cascade run runs at the conformance setting and is recorded
- *WHEN* the PM spawns a conformance run for a cascade
- *THEN* the run is at the Architect's conformance role default
- *AND* the change's record holds it as an Architect conformance agent

*** Scenario: A cascade run is recorded as applied without asking
- *WHEN* the PM has spawned a conformance run for a cascade
- *THEN* a =confirmation= ask records the run as applied without asking

*** Scenario: Review starvation fires when merged tasks wait on review
- *WHEN* the tasks merged and awaiting review, over the tasks being implemented, exceed the review-starvation threshold
- *THEN* the digest carries a ⚠ review-starvation signal
- *AND* it recommends slowing the Implementors' cadence or running more Reviewers in parallel

*** Scenario: Externalisation pressure fires on a growing cross-change backlog
- *WHEN* the cross-change backlog has grown in every cycle over the threshold's number of cycles
- *THEN* the digest carries an externalisation-pressure signal

*** Scenario: A coherent externalised cluster is proposed for promotion
- *WHEN* three or more externalised tasks share modules and a discovered class
- *THEN* the digest carries a candidate question whether to promote that cluster into the active change
- *AND* the PM does not promote it

*** Scenario: Priority inversion fires when little active work is on the main path
- *WHEN* fewer than three in ten of the active tasks are on the main path
- *THEN* the digest carries a ⚠ priority-inversion signal
- *AND* it recommends returning to the main path

*** Scenario: A task not done for too long is a stale signal
- *WHEN* a task has been in a state other than done for more cycles than the stale threshold
- *THEN* the digest carries a stale signal

*** Scenario: A task blocked for too long is a blocked-path signal
- *WHEN* a task has been blocked for the stale threshold's number of cycles or more
- *THEN* the digest carries a blocked-path stagnation signal

*** Scenario: A class completing at half the average rate is a cycle anti-pattern
- *WHEN* the completion rate of one task class is below half the rate across all tasks
- *THEN* the digest carries a cycle anti-pattern signal naming the class

*** Scenario: Recurring rejection reasons are a process signal and a candidate process ask
- *WHEN* three or more rejections share a reason within one Implementor's tasks or one task class
- *THEN* the digest carries a process signal naming the class
- *AND* it names the class as a candidate =process= ask

*** Scenario: A pause is recommended when the design looks wrong
- *WHEN* a cascade, throughput inversion and priority inversion all fire in the same cycle
- *THEN* the digest recommends pausing the change
- *AND* the PM does not pause it

** Requirement: Blocked work and open asks reach the digest's asks
:PROPERTIES:
:GOAL:     none stated
:END:
The digest SHALL list every open ask with its file, SHALL name the open ask a blocked task already
waits on instead of raising another, and SHALL turn every other blocker into a candidate question
the user can read without the code; the PM SHALL write no ask file. The digest SHALL count what was
settled without the user and what was recorded rather than asked.

*** Scenario: Every open ask is listed with its file
- *WHEN* an ask of the change is open
- *THEN* the digest's asks table has a row for it with its question, what it blocks, a recommendation and its file's path relative to the home

*** Scenario: A task blocked on an ask re-surfaces that ask
- *WHEN* a task is blocked on an open ask
- *THEN* the digest names that ask for the task
- *AND* no new candidate question is raised for it

*** Scenario: A task blocked on anything else is a candidate question
- *WHEN* a task is blocked on something other than an ask
- *THEN* the digest carries a candidate question for it

*** Scenario: A candidate question is plain, says what it blocks and recommends
- *WHEN* the digest carries a candidate question
- *THEN* it is a plain question for the reader the project's configuration names
- *AND* it says what it blocks and gives a recommendation

*** Scenario: The PM writes no ask file
- *WHEN* the PM writes its digest
- *THEN* it writes no ask file; its candidate questions are in the digest only

*** Scenario: The digest counts the asks applied without asking and the process notes
- *WHEN* a PM digest is written
- *THEN* it states how many asks of the cycle were applied without asking and how many process asks are in the inbox
- *AND* no process ask is put in it as a question

*** Scenario: The digest counts the findings by kind and the hardening list
- *WHEN* a PM digest is written
- *THEN* it states the findings by kind and how many entries are on the change's hardening list, naming the list's file

*** Scenario: The digest counts the record corrections
- *WHEN* the cycle made record corrections
- *THEN* the digest states how many

*** Scenario: A digest with nothing to ask has an empty asks table
- *WHEN* no ask is open and no candidate question is raised
- *THEN* the digest's asks table is empty and the digest is complete

** Requirement: On goal drift the PM recommends and the user decides
:PROPERTIES:
:GOAL:     G2 (O-NS-2)
:END:
The digest SHALL show the main path's completion over the history window, SHALL fire a goal-drift
signal when the main path stalls while other work goes on, and SHALL then carry a recommendation for
the intent with a one-line reason; the recommendation SHALL change nothing until the user decides.

*** Scenario: The goal-drift check shows the main path's completion
- *WHEN* a PM digest is written
- *THEN* its goal-drift check states the main path's completion ratio and how it has moved over the history window

*** Scenario: Goal drift fires when the main path stalls while other work completes
- *WHEN* the main path's completion ratio is flat or falling over the history window, tasks off the main path keep completing, and drainage stays positive
- *THEN* the digest carries a ⚠ goal-drift signal
- *AND* its goal-drift check recommends a course for the intent, with a one-line reason

*** Scenario: The goal-drift recommendation is one of the ask's options, or none
- *WHEN* the digest's goal-drift check is written
- *THEN* its recommendation is =revise=, =split=, =abandon= or =continue= when goal drift fired, and =none= when it did not

*** Scenario: The goal-drift recommendation changes nothing
- *WHEN* the digest recommends a course on goal drift
- *THEN* the PM changes neither the intent's goals nor its status line
- *AND* the course is the user's to decide

** Requirement: The PM acts only through its digest and the cascade run
:PROPERTIES:
:GOAL:     none stated
:END:
The PM SHALL change no code and no task's text, and SHALL spawn no Implementor or Reviewer; what it
changes in the change is its digest and, on a cascade, the conformance run it starts.

*** Scenario: The PM changes no code and no task's text
- *WHEN* the PM has run
- *THEN* the code root has no change of its making
- *AND* no task file's text has changed

*** Scenario: The PM writes nothing to the queue
- *WHEN* the PM has run
- *THEN* no task's labels, priority, class or blocker note has changed by its hand
- *AND* any re-prioritisation or relabelling it has in mind is a recommendation in the digest

*** Scenario: The PM spawns no Implementor or Reviewer
- *WHEN* the PM has run
- *THEN* the change's record holds no Implementor or Reviewer it spawned

** Requirement: The PM's role default
:PROPERTIES:
:GOAL:     none stated
:END:
The PM's agent pass SHALL run at its role default.

*** Scenario: The PM's agent pass runs at sonnet, medium
- *WHEN* the PM's agent pass is spawned
- *THEN* it runs at =sonnet=, =medium=

* Gaps

** Gap: Whether the asks table holds candidate questions, and with what file
:PROPERTIES:
:REQUIREMENT: Blocked work and open asks reach the digest's asks
:END:
The asks table's question column is the PM's candidate questions and its file column is rendered from
the open asks; a candidate question has no file yet when the digest is written.

** Gap: Whether a digest is written when the prose pass fails
:PROPERTIES:
:REQUIREMENT: Every number in the digest comes from the change's record
:END:
The role says the digest is useful with the computed part alone; the digest file is the prose pass's
output, and the integrate gate needs it with a non-empty Signals section.

#+STATUS: draft 2026-11-13, extracted from the reference skill (=main= =e356da7=); not yet reviewed; at the hand-off review 2026-11-13: G8 and G11 applied, its role default added; the recommended actions and the direction of a defect outside the work filled

* Purpose

A Reviewer reads one merged task, or at integrate the whole change, and writes what it found to a
review file, then hands back one line. This spec states what that file must carry, what makes a
finding (its kind, severity, direction, locations, evidence and recommended action, whoever writes
it, the Architect included), what a Reviewer may and may not touch while it probes the merge, and the
form of its hand-back line. It does not state what a Reviewer is given, when it is spawned, at which
agent setting, or what becomes of its findings: =execute= states those for a task's review and
=integrate= for the whole-change review.

* Requirements

** Requirement: The Reviewer writes one review file per review
:PROPERTIES:
:GOAL:     none stated
:END:
The Reviewer SHALL write its review of a task as one review file, at the path its brief names in the
cycle's directory, opening with what it reviewed and how many findings it wrote, followed by a
summary, the findings numbered in order, and its thoughts when it has any.

*** Scenario: The review file is at the path its brief names
- *WHEN* a Reviewer reviews a task
- *THEN* it writes its review file at =.orchestrator/changes/<change>/cycles/<cycle-id>/reviews/<task>.md= in the home, the path its brief names

*** Scenario: The review file opens with what was reviewed and what was found
- *WHEN* a review file is written
- *THEN* it opens with front matter holding =task_name=, =merge_commit=, =reviewed_at=, =finding_count= and =clean_review=
- *AND* =merge_commit= is the commit the Reviewer reviewed

*** Scenario: The finding count and the clean mark agree with the findings
- *WHEN* a review file is written
- *THEN* its =finding_count= is the number of findings in it
- *AND* its =clean_review= is true when it has no finding and false otherwise

*** Scenario: The review file holds a summary, the numbered findings and the thoughts
- *WHEN* a review file is written
- *THEN* it has a =## Summary= of one paragraph: what the Reviewer looked at, what it ruled out and what it found
- *AND* a =## Findings= section, each finding under its own heading =### Finding <n>: <one line>=, numbered from 1
- *AND* a =## Thoughts= section at the end only when the Reviewer has a thought

** Requirement: Every defect the Reviewer finds is a finding, whatever its size, likelihood or place
:PROPERTIES:
:GOAL:     none stated (=north-star.org= * Disposition states it; no goal does)
:END:
The Reviewer SHALL write every defect it finds in code or text that exists as a finding: an =issue=
when it can point at it, a =question= when it suspects it and cannot settle it. It SHALL report a
defect whatever it judges of its likelihood, its size or its place, leaving the weighing to the
routing; it SHALL report no matter of style or preference; and a review with no finding SHALL say
what was looked for.

*** Scenario: A defect the Reviewer can point at is an issue
- *WHEN* the Reviewer finds a defect it can point at in code or text that exists
- *THEN* the review file has a finding of kind =issue= for it, with a severity and its evidence

*** Scenario: A defect in code the diff does not change is still reported
- *WHEN* the Reviewer finds a defect in code the diff calls or is called by, or in the tests, and the diff does not change that code
- *THEN* it is a finding of kind =issue=
- *AND* it is reported though the Reviewer judges it unlikely, small, or off the change's main path

*** Scenario: A present defect is never only a thought
- *WHEN* a line the Reviewer would write names a defect present at the reviewed merge commit
- *THEN* it is a finding of kind =issue=, whatever its severity
- *AND* it is not a line among the thoughts

*** Scenario: A defect the file names anywhere is among its findings
- *WHEN* the review file's summary, its thoughts or what it ruled out names a defect in code or text that exists
- *THEN* that defect is also a finding of kind =issue= or =question= in the file's findings

*** Scenario: A document the diff makes untrue is a finding
- *WHEN* the diff makes a sentence untrue in a document (a README, a help text, a comment, a cited seam row or scenario), whether or not the diff touches the document
- *THEN* the sentence is a finding of kind =issue=

*** Scenario: A suspected defect the Reviewer cannot settle is a question
- *WHEN* the Reviewer suspects a defect and cannot settle whether it exists
- *THEN* it is a finding of kind =question=
- *AND* its evidence gives the reasoning, and the reproduction that would settle it

*** Scenario: A probe that shows a suspected defect makes it an issue
- *WHEN* the Reviewer settles a suspected defect with a probe and the probe shows the defect
- *THEN* it is a finding of kind =issue=
- *AND* the probe's output is in its evidence

*** Scenario: A probe that shows the code right leaves no finding
- *WHEN* the Reviewer settles a suspected defect with a probe and the probe shows the code right
- *THEN* the review file has no finding for it

*** Scenario: Style and preference are not findings
- *WHEN* what the Reviewer would say is a matter of naming taste, formatting, a structure it would have written differently to the same effect, or a decision already settled
- *THEN* the review file has no finding for it

*** Scenario: A review with no finding says what was looked for
- *WHEN* the Reviewer finds nothing
- *THEN* the review file says what the Reviewer looked for and why it ruled each thing out

*** Scenario: A pending marker left, a cited scenario's test untouched or an edit under docs is a finding
- *WHEN* the diff leaves a test it promotes still marked pending, or does not touch the test of a scenario the task cites, or edits the change's documents under =docs/=
- *THEN* each of these is a finding

*** Scenario: A wrong task scope is a spec signal, not a change of scope
- *WHEN* the Reviewer judges that the scope the task states is wrong
- *THEN* it writes a finding whose direction is =spec-signal=

** Requirement: A finding carries its labels, its place, its evidence and a recommended action
:PROPERTIES:
:GOAL:     none stated (=north-star.org= * Disposition states it; no goal does)
:END:
Each finding SHALL carry its kind, its severity, its locations, its evidence and a recommended action,
and a Reviewer's finding SHALL name the direction it looks in. A finding the user must settle SHALL
say what the thing in question is for and what it blocks, and a spec signal of kind =issue= SHALL
carry a stopgap. The reporter SHALL NOT route its own finding.

*** Scenario: Each finding carries a kind, a severity, its locations, its evidence and a recommended action
- *WHEN* a finding is written in a review file
- *THEN* it carries a =kind=, =issue= or =question=
- *AND* a =severity=, =high=, =medium= or =low=
- *AND* at least one location, a file and a line
- *AND* its evidence
- *AND* a recommended action: a kind, and one line saying what should happen

*** Scenario: A recommended action is an inline fix, a rework or the user's
- *WHEN* a finding's recommended action is written
- *THEN* its kind is =inline-fix=, =rework= or =route-to-user=

*** Scenario: Every finding says what it blocks
- *WHEN* a finding is written, in a review file or in a file of its own
- *THEN* it carries =blocks=: the task, a merge, the next plan or the change's done that waits on it, or =nothing=

*** Scenario: A finding about the change's work names the direction it looks in
- *WHEN* a finding is about the diff's own code, its drift from the change's design, or a spec or design the work shows may be wrong
- *THEN* its =direction= is =sub-par-code=, =design-drift= or =spec-signal= respectively

*** Scenario: A defect in code the change did not write is sub-par code
- *WHEN* a finding is about code or tests that exist and that the diff does not change
- *THEN* its =direction= is =sub-par-code=

*** Scenario: Severity is the harm when the defect triggers
- *WHEN* a finding's severity is set
- *THEN* it is =high= for wrong or lost data, a breach of security or isolation, or a failure on a use the change exists for
- *AND* =medium= for a wrong result or a misleading message that has a workaround
- *AND* =low= for a minor wrong output or an untrue comment

*** Scenario: An issue's evidence shows the defect and who it harms
- *WHEN* a finding is of kind =issue=
- *THEN* its evidence names the line, the run or the probe's output, and what goes wrong for whom

*** Scenario: A finding for the user says what the thing is for and what it blocks
- *WHEN* a finding recommends taking it to the user
- *THEN* it says in one sentence, in product terms, what the feature, flag or rule in question is for
- *AND* what it blocks: a task, a merge, the next plan, the change's done, or nothing

*** Scenario: A spec signal says what the thing is for and what it blocks
- *WHEN* a finding's direction is =spec-signal=
- *THEN* it says what the feature, flag or rule in question is for, and what it blocks

*** Scenario: A spec signal of kind issue carries a stopgap
- *WHEN* a finding with the =spec-signal= direction is of kind =issue=
- *THEN* it carries a stopgap: the least change that turns the defect's wrong success into a loud failure, whichever option the ask would offer
- *AND* when no change holds under every option, the stopgap is =none= with the reason

*** Scenario: The reporter leaves the route to the routing
- *WHEN* a Reviewer writes a finding
- *THEN* the finding names no route, neither blocking nor hardening
- *AND* its recommended action says what should be done were the defect fixed now

** Requirement: A thought is one line at the end of the review file
:PROPERTIES:
:GOAL:     none stated
:END:
The Reviewer SHALL write a speculative need, or cleanup that changes no behaviour today, as one line
labelled =thought= at the end of the review file, never as a finding.

*** Scenario: A speculative need or a cleanup is one thought line
- *WHEN* the Reviewer sees a need that would arise only after some future change, or cleanup that changes no behaviour today
- *THEN* it is one line labelled =thought= under the file's =## Thoughts=
- *AND* it is not a finding

** Requirement: The Reviewer changes nothing it reviews
:PROPERTIES:
:GOAL:     none stated
:END:
The Reviewer SHALL be read-only: it SHALL apply no fix, commit nothing and leave the task file as it
found it. It SHALL run its probes and the project's tests in the foreground, in the scratch
directory its brief names, against the merge's checkout, leave nothing behind, and leave alone what
other agents on the machine share.

*** Scenario: The Reviewer applies no fix and moves no ref
- *WHEN* a Reviewer hands back
- *THEN* no branch or other ref of the repository or of the home has moved
- *AND* the merge's checkout is clean at the reviewed merge commit

*** Scenario: The Reviewer leaves the task file alone
- *WHEN* a Reviewer hands back
- *THEN* the task file is as it was when the Reviewer was spawned

*** Scenario: Probes run in the scratch directory the brief names, which is gone afterwards
- *WHEN* the Reviewer runs a probe
- *THEN* the probe runs in the scratch directory its brief names, against the merge's checkout
- *AND* that directory is gone when the Reviewer hands back

*** Scenario: Every run ends before the hand-back
- *WHEN* the Reviewer runs the project's tests or a probe
- *THEN* the run is in the foreground and has ended before the Reviewer hands back
- *AND* the Reviewer hands back once

*** Scenario: The Reviewer leaves what other agents share alone
- *WHEN* the Reviewer probes on a machine other agents share
- *THEN* it uses no =git stash=, and prunes, collects or removes no worktree it did not create
- *AND* any server or container it starts carries the name of the review's checkout, not a fixed one
- *AND* it starts no GUI or interactive program and stops no process it did not start

** Requirement: The Reviewer hands back one line of counts
:PROPERTIES:
:GOAL:     none stated
:END:
The Reviewer SHALL hand back one line of at most 200 characters, naming the task and counting its
findings by kind and its spec signals and thoughts, with no path and nothing else.

*** Scenario: The hand-back line counts the findings
- *WHEN* the Reviewer has written its review file
- *THEN* it hands back one line, =reviewed <task> issues=<n> questions=<n> spec-signal=<n> thoughts=<n>=, of at most 200 characters
- *AND* =issues= and =questions= count the file's findings of each kind, and =spec-signal= those among them whose direction is =spec-signal=

*** Scenario: The hand-back carries no path and no summary
- *WHEN* the Reviewer hands back
- *THEN* the line names no path
- *AND* the hand-back holds nothing but that line

** Requirement: The whole-change review is written in the same form
:PROPERTIES:
:GOAL:     G11
:END:
The Reviewer of the whole change SHALL write one review file for the cycle in the form of a task's,
report what no task's review could see, and hand back one line.

*** Scenario: The whole-change review is one review file of its cycle
- *WHEN* the whole-change review runs
- *THEN* its review file is =.orchestrator/changes/<change>/cycles/<cycle-id>/reviews/whole-change.md= in the home, in the form of a task's review file
- *AND* the Reviewer hands back one line

*** Scenario: The whole-change review names the whole change in place of a task
- *WHEN* the whole-change review file is written
- *THEN* its =task_name= is =whole-change= and its =merge_commit= the change's HEAD it reviewed
- *AND* its hand-back line is =reviewed whole-change issues=<n> questions=<n> spec-signal=<n> thoughts=<n>=

*** Scenario: The whole-change review reports what no task's review could see
- *WHEN* tasks of the change interact wrongly, or a change bypassed review, or a scenario's test passes while the surface the user would use does not
- *THEN* the whole-change review file has a finding for it

** Requirement: An Architect's finding is a file of its own, with the same labels
:PROPERTIES:
:GOAL:     none stated (=north-star.org= * Disposition states it; no goal does)
:END:
Each finding the Architect reports SHALL be one file in its cycle's directory, carrying the kind and
severity every finding carries, its class and trigger, where it was found and what it is about, why
the tests missed it and a recommended resolution; the Architect SHALL write no decoration, and a
thought SHALL never be a finding file.

*** Scenario: An Architect's finding is one file in its cycle's directory
- *WHEN* the Architect reports a finding
- *THEN* it is one file, =.orchestrator/changes/<change>/cycles/<cycle-id>/findings/<finding-id>.md= in the home
- *AND* its id is =arch-<cycle-id>-<n>=

*** Scenario: An Architect's finding carries its labels in its front matter
- *WHEN* an Architect's finding file is written
- *THEN* its front matter holds =finding_id=, =trigger= (=premise-check= or =conformance=), =kind=, =severity=, =class=, a =title= of at most 80 characters, =discovered_from=, =discovered_by: architect=, =discovered_at=, the =seam= it is about when there is one, and =evidence=
- *AND* its =class= is one of =shape-fragmentation=, =vocabulary-mismatch=, =responsibility-leakage=, =dead-branch=, =interface-drift=, =mutation=, =invariant-gap= or =duplication=
- *AND* the Architect writes no =decoration=

*** Scenario: An Architect's finding says where, why the tests missed it and what to do
- *WHEN* an Architect's finding file is written
- *THEN* it has a =## Locations= section, each a file and line with its context
- *AND* a =## Why tests missed it= section of one sentence
- *AND* a =## Recommended resolution= section of one paragraph that names files and functions, and names the seam row amended and how when the code is right and the row was stale

*** Scenario: An Architect's thought is never a finding file
- *WHEN* the Architect has a thought
- *THEN* no finding file is written for it

** Requirement: The Reviewer's role default
:PROPERTIES:
:GOAL:     none stated
:END:
The Reviewer SHALL run at its role default unless a task override raises it.

*** Scenario: The Reviewer's role default is opus, high for a task and xhigh for the whole change
- *WHEN* a Reviewer is spawned at its role default
- *THEN* a task's Reviewer runs at =opus=, =high=
- *AND* the whole-change Reviewer at =opus=, =xhigh=

* Gaps

** Gap: What read-only allows when the tests write in the checkout
:PROPERTIES:
:REQUIREMENT: The Reviewer changes nothing it reviews
:END:
The Reviewer may run the project's tests against the merge's checkout, and nothing a probe writes may
enter the repository; the reference skill does not say whether files a test run leaves in the
checkout (caches, build output) break the read-only contract.

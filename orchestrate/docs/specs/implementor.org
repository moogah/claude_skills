#+TITLE: implementor
#+OPTIONS: ^:{} -:nil
#+STATUS: draft 2026-11-13, extracted from the reference skill (=main= =e356da7=); not yet reviewed; six gaps open

* Purpose

The Implementor is the role agent that does one task of a batch, in the task's own worktree, from
the brief the execute phase gives it. This spec states what its work leaves behind: the one commit on
the task's branch, the Observations and Discoveries it appends to the task file in the home, the
report it writes for the phase agent, and the one line it hands back; and what it leaves alone on a
machine other agents share. It does not state when an Implementor is spawned, what its brief holds,
what becomes of its commit, its notes or its report, or how a wrong premise, a discovery or a rework
is routed: those are =execute='s.

* Requirements

** Requirement: The task's work is one commit on its task's branch
:PROPERTIES:
:GOAL:     none stated
:END:
The Implementor SHALL leave its task's work as one commit on its task's branch, with the subject
=Implement task <task>: <description>=, made once the task's verification passes, promoting every
pending test the task names and correcting any sentence the work made untrue. The commit SHALL hold
nothing of the change's documents and nothing of the task file. The Implementor SHALL fix no defect
outside its task, and SHALL declare any work beyond it.

*** Scenario: The task's work is one commit with a fixed subject
- *WHEN* an Implementor hands back =done=
- *THEN* its task's branch has exactly one commit beyond the point it was cut from
- *AND* that commit's subject is =Implement task <task>: <description>=

*** Scenario: The commit passes the task's verification
- *WHEN* the Implementor commits its work
- *THEN* the task's verification command passes on that commit

*** Scenario: The commit promotes the pending tests the task names
- *WHEN* the task's Verification section names pending tests
- *THEN* the commit removes the pending marker from each of them

*** Scenario: The commit leaves the change's documents alone
- *WHEN* the Implementor commits its work
- *THEN* the commit changes nothing under =docs/=: no seam row, no scenario, no design or intent text
- *AND* this holds when the Implementor finds a cited seam row or scenario wrong

*** Scenario: The task file stays out of the commit
- *WHEN* the Implementor commits its work
- *THEN* the commit holds neither the task file nor its Observations and Discoveries
- *AND* this holds when the home is the code root

*** Scenario: A sentence the work made untrue is corrected in the same commit
- *WHEN* the work makes a docstring, a comment, a README sentence or a help text untrue
- *THEN* the task's commit corrects that sentence

*** Scenario: A defect outside the task is not fixed in the task's commit
- *WHEN* the Implementor finds a defect in code or text that exists outside its task
- *THEN* its commit leaves that defect as it was

*** Scenario: Work beyond the task is declared
- *WHEN* the commit changes something the task does not ask for, or departs from the approach the task prescribes
- *THEN* the report's =deviation_summary= says so
- *AND* the hand-back carries =deviated=
- *AND* an Observation names the departure with its evidence

*** Scenario: The Implementor moves no branch but its task's
- *WHEN* an Implementor hands back
- *THEN* the change's integration branch and every other branch but its task's are where they were when it was spawned

** Requirement: The Implementor's notes are appended to the task file, in two sections
:PROPERTIES:
:GOAL:     none stated
:END:
The Implementor SHALL append =## Observations= and =## Discoveries= to its task file in the home, at
the path its brief names. Observations SHALL hold only departures from the prescribed approach,
weak tests, ambiguities it resolved, wrong premises the work absorbed, what it saw of other agents
on the machine, and thoughts; a
defect in code or text that exists outside the task SHALL never be only an Observation.

*** Scenario: The notes are appended to the task file in the home
- *WHEN* an Implementor hands back =done=
- *THEN* the task file in the home, at the path its brief named, has a =## Observations= section and a =## Discoveries= section appended to it

*** Scenario: An Observation is one of its kinds
- *WHEN* the Implementor writes an Observation other than a seam objection (an open gap)
- *THEN* it is one of: a departure from the task's prescribed approach, with its evidence; a test that passes but asserts weakly, or a fake that diverges from the real thing; a contradiction or ambiguity in the spec or design that the work made it resolve; a wrong premise the work absorbed; a =thought:=; a stray process; or a failure that passed on a rerun

*** Scenario: A speculative need is one Observation labelled thought
- *WHEN* the Implementor sees a need that would arise only under a future change
- *THEN* it is one line in Observations, labelled =thought:=

*** Scenario: A defect outside the task is never only an Observation
- *WHEN* an Observation names a defect in code or text that exists outside the task
- *THEN* the Discoveries hold an entry for that defect

*** Scenario: A process the Implementor did not start is noted, not stopped
- *WHEN* the Implementor finds a stray process it did not start
- *THEN* an Observation names it
- *AND* the process is left running

*** Scenario: A failure that passes on a rerun is noted with both results
- *WHEN* a test fails, then passes when run again, while other agents' runs were going on
- *THEN* an Observation says so, with both results

** Requirement: Every defect outside the task is a discovery, whatever its size
:PROPERTIES:
:GOAL:     none stated (=north-star.org= * Disposition states it; no goal does)
:END:
Every defect the Implementor finds in code or text that exists outside its task SHALL be one
Discoveries entry, of kind =issue= or =question=, with a severity and evidence, its id listed under
the report's =candidate_tasks=, however small or unlikely it looks. Each Discoveries entry SHALL
carry its id, a class from a closed set, a description, its kind, severity and evidence, and a
recommendation. The Implementor SHALL write no task file.

*** Scenario: A defect outside the task is a discovery, however small
- *WHEN* the Implementor finds a defect in code beside its task or in a document, outside what the task covers
- *THEN* the Discoveries hold one entry for it, of kind =issue= or =question=, with its severity and its evidence
- *AND* the entry is there whatever the Implementor judges of the defect's size or likelihood

*** Scenario: A discovered defect is listed among the report's candidate tasks
- *WHEN* the Discoveries hold an entry for a defect outside the task
- *THEN* the report's =candidate_tasks= lists that entry's id

*** Scenario: A Discoveries entry carries its fields
- *WHEN* the Implementor writes a Discoveries entry
- *THEN* it has a =discovery_id= of the form =disc-<task>-<n>=, a =description=, an =evidence= and a =recommendation=
- *AND* a =class= that is one of =shape-fragmentation=, =vocabulary-mismatch=, =responsibility-leakage=, =dead-branch=, =interface-drift=, =mutation=, =invariant-gap=, =spec-signal=, =deviation=, =scope-question= or =duplication=
- *AND* a =kind= and a =severity= of =high=, =medium= or =low=
- *AND* an =affected_seam= when the entry concerns a seam

*** Scenario: A suspected defect names the reproduction that would settle it
- *WHEN* a Discoveries entry is of kind =question=
- *THEN* its evidence names the reproduction that would settle it

*** Scenario: A discovery only the user can settle says what it blocks
- *WHEN* a Discoveries entry is of class =spec-signal= or =scope-question= and only the user can settle it
- *THEN* the entry says what the feature is for and what the entry blocks, besides its kind and evidence

*** Scenario: A scope question no goal needs is a thought
- *WHEN* the Implementor's scope question concerns something no goal of the intent needs
- *THEN* it is a =thought:= line in Observations
- *AND* no Discoveries entry is written for it

*** Scenario: The Implementor writes no task file
- *WHEN* the Implementor finds a defect outside its task
- *THEN* no task file is added to the home

** Requirement: A cited seam row the code shows wrong is objected to, not reproduced
:PROPERTIES:
:GOAL:     none stated
:END:
When the work shows a cited seam row wrong, the Implementor SHALL implement what the code needs,
SHALL record a seam objection as a Discoveries entry naming the row, and SHALL list it in its report.

*** Scenario: A seam row the work shows wrong gets a seam objection
- *WHEN* the work shows that a cited seam row is wrong for the code
- *THEN* the commit implements what the code needs, not what the row says
- *AND* a Discoveries entry names the row as its =affected_seam=, with a class that says how it is wrong

*** Scenario: The report lists each seam objection
- *WHEN* the Implementor has made a seam objection
- *THEN* the report's =push_backs= names the seam, with a one-line summary

** Requirement: A wrong premise, or a failing test the task did not write, is settled by what it changes
:PROPERTIES:
:GOAL:     none stated
:END:
Before writing code the Implementor SHALL check the claims its task and the task's cited rows make
about the code at its worktree's HEAD, for every task. A wrong premise the task can absorb, and a
test the task did not write that is wrong without the behaviour being at stake, SHALL be settled in
the work and declared; one that changes what the task should do SHALL stop the task with a
=blocked= hand-back naming the premise.

*** Scenario: A wrong premise the task can absorb is corrected in the work
- *WHEN* a claim of the task about the code is wrong in a name, a path, a signature or work already done
- *THEN* the work corrects for it
- *AND* an Observation names the claim and what HEAD has

*** Scenario: A wrong premise that changes the task stops it
- *WHEN* a claim of the task is wrong in a way that changes what the task should do: the behaviour it adds is there already, it contradicts a cited row or scenario, or the work belongs to another seam
- *THEN* the Implementor hands back =blocked <task>: premise wrong, <the claim>=
- *AND* its report states the claim and what HEAD has
- *AND* this holds whether or not the Architect checked the task's premises at plan

*** Scenario: A failing test the task did not write, against wrong code, is settled in the code
- *WHEN* a test the task did not write fails against the work and the code is what is wrong
- *THEN* the commit fixes the code

*** Scenario: A wrong test is changed only when the stated behaviour holds, and declared
- *WHEN* a test the task did not write fails against the work, the test is what is wrong, and a change to it leaves the behaviour stated by the cited rows and scenarios intact
- *THEN* the commit changes the test
- *AND* the report's =deviation_summary= says so and the hand-back carries =deviated=
- *AND* a Discoveries entry of class =deviation= quotes the assertion before and after

*** Scenario: A test whose only fix changes stated behaviour stops the task
- *WHEN* a test the task did not write fails against the work and any fix would change behaviour that a cited row or scenario states
- *THEN* the Implementor hands back =blocked <task>: premise wrong, <the claim>=

** Requirement: The report carries the task's account for the phase agent
:PROPERTIES:
:GOAL:     none stated
:END:
The Implementor SHALL write its report at the path its brief names, in a fixed form whose shape the
project's own additions to the role cannot change, with counts that match the task file's sections.

*** Scenario: The report is written with its fields
- *WHEN* an Implementor hands back =done=
- *THEN* a report is at the path its brief named
- *AND* it holds =task_name=, =commit_sha= (the task's commit), =test_output_tail= (the last ten lines of the test command's output), =observations_count=, =discoveries_count=, =candidate_tasks= and =push_backs=
- *AND* =deviation_summary= when the work deviated

*** Scenario: The report's counts match the task file
- *WHEN* the report is written
- *THEN* =observations_count= is the number of bullets in the task file's Observations and =discoveries_count= the number of its Discoveries entries, each 0 when there are none

*** Scenario: The project's additions to the role leave the report's form alone
- *WHEN* the project adds its own text to the Implementor's role
- *THEN* the report keeps its fields, and the Observations and Discoveries stay two sections with their own contents

*** Scenario: A fix for a defect shown on a real surface is pinned there, or says why not
- *WHEN* the Implementor's task is to fix a review finding shown by a probe of a real surface: a running process, a daemon, a client, =git= or the file system
- *THEN* the test that pins the fix drives that surface and reproduces the probe
- *AND* a test against a fake of the surface is not the pin
- *AND* when the tests cannot drive the surface, the report and an Observation say so

** Requirement: The Implementor hands back one line, once
:PROPERTIES:
:GOAL:     none stated
:END:
The Implementor SHALL end by handing back one line of at most 200 characters, once, after its
commit and its test run: =done= with the task and the commit, flagged when it deviated or listed
candidate tasks, or =blocked= or =failed= with one clause. The line SHALL name no path and carry no
report.

*** Scenario: A done hand-back names the task and its commit
- *WHEN* an Implementor finishes with a commit
- *THEN* its last output is one line of at most 200 characters, =done <task> <sha>=, the sha being its commit on the task's branch

*** Scenario: A deviation is flagged in the hand-back
- *WHEN* the report's =deviation_summary= is not empty
- *THEN* the =done= line carries =deviated=

*** Scenario: Candidate tasks are counted in the hand-back
- *WHEN* the report's =candidate_tasks= is not empty
- *THEN* the =done= line carries =candidates=<n>=, =n= being the number of ids it lists

*** Scenario: The hand-back names no path and carries no report
- *WHEN* an Implementor hands back
- *THEN* the line names no file's path
- *AND* holds nothing of the report, the test output, the Observations or the Discoveries

*** Scenario: A task that needs revision stops the Implementor with a blocked line
- *WHEN* the task itself appears to need revision before it can be implemented
- *THEN* the Implementor implements no guess at it
- *AND* hands back =blocked <task>: <what it observed and what it blocks>=, in one clause
- *AND* writes no ask file

*** Scenario: The Implementor hands back once, after its commit and its test run
- *WHEN* an Implementor hands back
- *THEN* no test run it started is still running
- *AND* it hands back no second time

** Requirement: The Implementor leaves what other agents share as it found it
:PROPERTIES:
:GOAL:     none stated
:END:
On a machine where other Implementors, Reviewers and test runs work at the same time, the
Implementor SHALL use only its own worktree and branch, the task file and report its brief names,
and its scratch directory, and SHALL leave the repository's shared lists, other agents' files and
processes, and any resource in use by another, as they were.

*** Scenario: Work is set aside with a commit, never a stash
- *WHEN* the Implementor needs to set work aside
- *THEN* it does so with a commit on its task's branch, folded into the task's one commit
- *AND* the repository's stash list is as it was when the Implementor was spawned

*** Scenario: The Implementor creates no worktree
- *WHEN* an Implementor runs
- *THEN* it works in the worktree it was given and creates none

*** Scenario: The repository's worktrees and objects are left alone
- *WHEN* an Implementor runs
- *THEN* it prunes no worktree, removes none it did not create, and runs no garbage collection of the repository

*** Scenario: Scratch files stay in the scratch directory the brief names
- *WHEN* the Implementor writes a scratch file
- *THEN* it is under the scratch directory its brief names
- *AND* it edits or deletes no scratch file outside it, including one at the top level of a scratchpad it shares with the agent that spawned it

*** Scenario: A container or server is named after the task's branch
- *WHEN* the Implementor starts a compose project or a server
- *THEN* it is named after its worktree's branch, never a fixed name
- *AND* a port or socket already in use is left to whoever holds it

*** Scenario: The Implementor starts no interactive program and stops only its own processes
- *WHEN* an Implementor runs
- *THEN* it starts no GUI or interactive program
- *AND* stops no process it did not start

** Requirement: The Implementor's role default
:PROPERTIES:
:GOAL:     none stated
:END:
The Implementor's role default SHALL be the model =opus= at =medium= effort.

*** Scenario: The Implementor's role default is opus at medium effort
- *WHEN* an Implementor is spawned for a task the plan wrote no override for
- *THEN* it runs at =opus=, =medium=

* Gaps

** Gap: Whether a seam objection is also an Observation
:PROPERTIES:
:REQUIREMENT: A cited seam row the code shows wrong is objected to, not reproduced
:END:
The role puts a seam objection in Discoveries; the task file's own description of its Observations
lists objections to cited seam rows among them. Whether an objection is written in both sections or
in Discoveries only is not settled.

** Gap: When an Implementor hands back failed rather than blocked
:PROPERTIES:
:REQUIREMENT: The Implementor hands back one line, once
:END:
The hand-back has a =failed <task>: <why>= form and no condition that chooses it over =blocked=.

** Gap: What a blocked Implementor leaves on its branch and in its report
:PROPERTIES:
:REQUIREMENT: A wrong premise, or a failing test the task did not write, is settled by what it changes
:END:
A =blocked= hand-back on a wrong premise comes with a report stating the claim. The reference skill
does not say whether the Implementor commits the work it has before stopping, nor what the report's
=commit_sha= and the task file's sections hold when it does not. =execute= fails a task whose
Implementor commits nothing, and blocks one whose Implementor hands back a wrong premise.

** Gap: A cited spec or design sentence the work made untrue
:PROPERTIES:
:REQUIREMENT: The task's work is one commit on its task's branch
:END:
Before its commit the Implementor re-reads the cited spec and design sentences with the rest of the
prose beside its change, and corrects in its commit any sentence its work made untrue; but that
commit may change nothing under =docs/=. What the Implementor leaves for such a sentence is not
stated.

** Gap: What the Implementor does with a task flagged as possibly obsolete
:PROPERTIES:
:REQUIREMENT: The Implementor hands back one line, once
:END:
A task file may carry an obsolescence flag whose ask is unanswered, telling its reader not to start
work. No hand-back or record is named for an Implementor given such a task.

** Gap: Whether the Implementor leaves its worktree clean
:PROPERTIES:
:REQUIREMENT: The task's work is one commit on its task's branch
:END:
The reference skill says the work is committed in one commit, and not that nothing is left
uncommitted or untracked in the worktree when the Implementor hands back. =implementor-ns12= checks
that the worktree is clean.
